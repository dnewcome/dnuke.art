parameters

Parameters explores the mind-body connection through digital imagery and material processes. Selected works form both a retrospective of the artist's digital past and glimpses of an uncertain future of physical form through automation.

The room above is the size of the 20-foot container at Hayes Valley Art Works where the show hung. The hang is not the show's. Walking in from the door, 2013 is on your left: blitbomb, a live video performance, and the rig it took to see it. The 2026 prints are on your right, and Reaction is on the back wall: one image sweeping an entire parameter space.

Every work carries its story. Click or tap one and it opens with the recipe that made it: the generator, the seed, the parameters. On a headset, press Enter VR; point and pull the trigger at the floor to move, or at a work to read it.

It runs on vr-gallery, an open WebGL/WebXR gallery. The room is one small file (gallery.yaml), and the same page can show any room described that way.
