thread

Thread is an interactive light sculpture. Twelve strings of LEDs are drawn between two pedestals and twist around each other into a single braided form.

Each pedestal has buttons. Press one and a comet runs out along your strand toward the other side. When a player at the far end answers, the two meet in the middle and the strand locks into a connected state. When every strand is connected, the whole thread lights up.

The render above is driven from the fabrication model. Every one of the 7,200 points is an LED in the real piece, at its real position, and the patterns run in the browser the same way they run on the sculpture.

  • 7,200 addressable LEDs
  • 12 strings, 600 LEDs each
  • 2 interactive pedestals
  • 48 Art-Net universes
  • steel, aluminum, addressable LED, Raspberry Pi
