thread

Thread is an interactive light sculpture by Sam Brocchini, built with a group of artists, engineers, and friends. Twelve strings of LEDs are drawn between two pedestals and twist around each other into a single braided form.

Each pedestal has buttons. Press one and a comet runs out along your strand toward the other side. When a player at the far end answers, the two meet in the middle and the strand locks into a connected state. When every strand is connected, the whole thread lights up.

The render above is driven from the fabrication model. Every one of the 7,200 points is an LED in the real piece, at its real position, facing the way its chip faces. LEDs pointed away from you dim, and strands hide behind the form and behind each other as it turns, so what you see is close to what the eye sees from that side of the piece. Toggle facing, strands and core to compare with a bare point cloud.
